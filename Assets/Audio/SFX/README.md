# Tavern gameplay sound effects

Original synthesized sounds created for FoodIsekaiZ. No external recordings or licensed samples are used.
48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV; shared headroom and softened attacks. Generated with seeded string excitation, damped resonators, metallic bell partials and filtered rustle.

Cues follow GameSoundCue order. Food pickup uses a plate tap/rustle boosted by 6 dB; accepted service resolves upward; wrong food uses a low wooden double tap. Visible order panels use a short paper flick with a muted wooden tick. Only the first emoji popup per NPC uses an audible low water bubble; face changes and later re-shows stay silent. Menu dismissal is silent. A short dry pop boosted by 3.5 dB plays when the successful-order particle burst starts after collapse, with a 0.18-second shared cooldown. Distinct NPC reveals queue up to six bubbles with 0.12-second spacing, instead of muting the second NPC; menu sounds have a separate 0.6-second cooldown. Payout, collection and banking use different coin clusters. Expiry uses an audible descending bell pair with playback priority. Uncollected piles use a short coin rattle when their reminder animation starts; simultaneous reminders share a 0.35-second cooldown; wave opening, break and completion have separate musical cadences. TimeWarning plays once at 10 and 5 seconds remaining.

Scene object Gameplay Sound Effects holds an independent 2D pool of six voices, master volume 0.65 and per-cue cooldowns. A dedicated BGM source lives on the same Gameplay Sound Effects object at volume 0.18, separate from the six effect voices. No sound plays for an empty bank visit or an unsuccessful pickup.

Generation source: Tools/Audio/GenerateTavernSounds.py (Python standard library; regenerates these authored assets).

Perk cues are generated by Tools/Audio/GeneratePerkSounds.py. The Big Perk shop keeps the Perk* tavern cues (reveal, focus, hold tick, cancel, unavailable, purchase). The Small Perk shop uses its own SmallPerk* anime food-reaction set: harp glissando reveal, two-note glockenspiel focus, a celesta hold tick that PerkShopPresentation pitches up a major third per step, a soft two-note cancel, two soft descending notes (E4 to C4, rounded tone with a slight pitch droop) when coins are short, mixed at -18 dB and paired with a floor-zone shake and a short glockenspiel confirm (kept brief because the card flip starts on the same frame). The Small purchase animation cues follow the same style with one idea each: PerkFlip (one air sweep with rising glockenspiel notes), PerkImpact (timpani, brass hit on IV and cymbal), PerkGather ("aah" choir swelling from IV to V under a cymbal swell), PerkCharge (held choir, accelerating timpani roll, a two-octave pitch riser and a reversed cymbal that peaks exactly on the release) and PerkRelease (a noise crack, falling sub-bass drop and doubled timpani under a late-entering orchestral V-to-I brass and choir chord, crash cymbal and a four-note glockenspiel cascade). These cues are matched by short-term loudness (300 ms, approximate K-weighting) instead of peak: feedback ticks sit at -20 to -23 dB (except the -18 dB unavailable warning), reveal, purchase and flip at about -18 to -19 dB, and the animation climbs from impact (-14) through gather (-16) and charge (-16) to release (about -11.7, the loudest cue), with a gentle soft limiter at 0.89. Anime cues use a 0.5 ms fade-in so the release transient stays sharp.

LunarFootstepLeft, LunarFootstepRight, LunarFootstepLeft2 and LunarFootstepRight2 are generated by Tools/Audio/GenerateLunarFootsteps.py: a small, light boot on old floorboards, made by exciting a bank of damped board resonances (150-1700 Hz) with a short noise burst, plus a faint joist boom, a tiny heel click and a softer toe tap, peaking at 0.5. Four board tunings keep consecutive steps from repeating. NpcGuidePresentation cycles them on Lunar's own AudioSource each time her walking bob lands, at volume 0.6 (just above the music) scaled by her distance from the standing spot down to 15% off screen, so walking in grows louder and walking out fades away, and plays GuideBubble there when her speech bubble opens.

Ready*, GuideBubble, CurtainClose and CurtainOpen are generated by Tools/Audio/GenerateIntroSounds.py with the same tavern instruments as the original set (wooden taps, metallic bells, plucked lute strings, cloth rustle and the water bubble) and the same light two-tap room reflection. They play over IntroMusic, which is in B minor, so pitched notes come only from B minor / D major; GuideBubble also plays over the E minor game BGM, so it stays an unpitched bubble. ReadyCardAppear is a paper flick and wooden tick that PlayerReadySelection pitches D-E-F#-G as the four number cards appear; ReadyStepOn is two rising bells; ReadyStepOff two soft falling wooden notes; ReadyHoldTick a wooden tick stepped F#-A-B through the 2-second hold; ReadyContested the WrongFood double tap moved into B minor; ReadyConfirm follows FoodServed: a wooden tap as the card squeezes, a rising D-F#-A bell chime and a small high bell sparkle as the Ready badge pops in at about 0.42 s. CurtainClose is cloth rustle swelling with the closing panels, then a soft muffled cloth puff and a short, quiet wooden tap as they meet at 0.63 s, about 4 dB over the rustle with nothing pitched ringing on; CurtainOpen is rustle easing away over a rising lute and a small bell at the reveal. Short-term loudness targets: step on -18, hold tick -20, contested -18, confirm -16, card ticks and step off -22, GuideBubble -23, CurtainClose -20 and CurtainOpen -21. FoodIsekaiZBgmPlayer ducks the music to 45% while a guide voice line plays.

Nothing cuts off hard at a scene change: the music fades to 35% while the MealMenuTransition page covers the wall and rises back as it lifts (0.5 s each way), intro music and game BGM fade in over 2 s and the intro music fades out over 2 s, GameSoundPlayer.StopCue fades the stopped voice over 0.25 s, and a guide voice line cut short by the guide leaving fades over 0.3 s.

Mix balance: effects sit clearly above the music. The game BGM plays at 0.16 and IntroMusic at 0.18 (both mp3 imports have Normalize off, so these volumes give the same loudness), Gameplay Sound Effects plays at master volume 1, and player feedback cues in the perk shop and Ready selection (focus, hold tick, cancel, unavailable, purchase and the Ready step/tick/contested/confirm cues) get a further +3 dB playerFeedbackGainDb. In game the music peaks near -28 dB short-term while player feedback lands between -15 and -20 dB; every cue still peaks at or below 0.89. Scene ambience stays only just above the music instead: CurtainClose and CurtainOpen use curtainGainDb -6 (about -26/-27 dB in game), and Lunar's footsteps (0.6, fading with distance) and GuideBubble (0.6) land 1-2 dB over the music. Lunar's voice lines (Assets/Voice/Lunar, Normalize off) peak near -15 dB short-term, level with the player feedback cues and about 20 dB over the ducked music: the intro line's AudioSource plays at 0.55 and the perk line at 0.69 on the NPC00-Lunar prefab.
